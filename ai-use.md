# Prototype 1 — AI Use

Answer all five questions, each in its own paragraph. AI use is optional. Honest
disclosure and reflection are required, and a no-AI submission can earn full
credit. Support every answer with something specific from your own work: a file
or function name, a design choice, a prompt, an error message, a test result, or
a commit.

## 1. Disclosure and scope

State whether you used AI on this assignment. If you did, name the tool (and
the model, if you know it) and say which parts of the work it touched. If you
didn't, say so and describe the resources or process you used instead.

## 2. A decision you made

Describe one real design or implementation decision. If you used AI, summarize
the prompt or advice and say what you accepted, changed, or rejected. If you
didn't, explain the alternatives you considered and why you chose this one.

## 3. Verification and trust

Name one technical or design claim you checked yourself, explain how you
checked it, and cite the evidence. An AI's own explanation doesn't count as
verification.

## 4. Revision or debugging

Describe one suggestion, attempt, or assumption that turned out wrong,
incomplete, or unhelpful. Explain how you noticed, what you changed, and what
showed that the fix worked.

## 5. Learning and reflection

Explain one thing you understand now that you didn't at the start, using an
example from this project, and say what you'll do differently next time.
